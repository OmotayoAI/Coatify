Questions

Before you message us.

Do I need to bring the car to you?

No. Send your location on WhatsApp and we'll arrange a pickup — from your home, office, or wherever the car currently is in Lagos.

How is the price worked out?

We inspect the car (in person or from photos you send) and give you a fixed price before any work starts. Nothing gets added once it's at the shop without telling you first.

Do you fit genuine or aftermarket parts?

Both — it depends what you want to spend. We'll tell you the options and the price difference before fitting anything, and we'll show you the old part once it's off the car.

Who actually does the work?

Painters, panel beaters and mechanics we've personally inspected — Coatify manages the job, pickup, and drop-off, but the hands-on work happens at their shops.

What if I'm outside your usual coverage areas?

Message us your location anyway. We cover most of Lagos and can often still arrange a pickup outside the areas listed above.
